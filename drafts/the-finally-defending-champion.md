## From pole, but not into the lead

After the qualifying where the Ferraris and the McLarens showed their strength around this track, the defending champion [Lando Norris](/drivers/lando-norris) was starting from [pole](/series/f1/weekend/11). The grid penalties of [Antonelli](/drivers/kimi-antonelli) and [Hamilton](/drivers/lewis-hamilton) left [Charles Leclerc](/drivers/charles-leclerc) to complete the front row. But the lead of the race wasn't earned by either of them but by the [McLaren](/teams/mclaren) of [Oscar Piastri](/drivers/oscar-piastri), who launched himself from P3 to the top at the first turns of the race. A great start was also made by [Max Verstappen](/drivers/max-verstappen). The 4-time world champion took advantage of the grid penalties and, starting from P4, made the move on Leclerc's [Ferrari](/teams/ferrari) to climb onto the podium. Some problem on [Russell](/drivers/george-russell)'s [Mercedes](/teams/mercedes) put the engine on anti-stall, making the British driver's start a total disaster as he fell all the way to the bottom.

## The first stops

In the next laps we got what we expected, a strategic, few-overtake race, the exception was Russell, who got some positions back trying to save his race. Verstappen was holding the Ferraris (with the soft compound) behind him as the McLaren at the top was creating space from the rest. Norris was complaining to the team that his teammate was much slower and he was wasting his time. This was happening until lap 13, when Ferrari decided to pit Hamilton in an attempt to undercut Verstappen. In his outlap the 7-time world champion had to clear both of the RBs and the Red Bull of [Isack Hadjar](/drivers/isack-hadjar) to increase his possibilities to step on the podium. At turn 5 he cleared [Lindblad](/drivers/arvid-lindblad) round the outside with some incredible driving using his fresh rubber. On the next lap Max was called in to avoid getting undercut, something that didn't happen as he rejoined behind Hamilton.

## The overtake of the season

The race continued with a retirement in the pit lane by [Bottas](/drivers/valtteri-bottas), whose brakes caught fire, this incident delayed Piastri's pit stop by a lap. All this back and forth between Hamilton and Verstappen resulted in the highlight of the race: on the start-finish straight both drivers were clearing [Lawson](/drivers/liam-lawson), when Max decided to dive-bomb Lewis at turn 1 from miles back, gaining the position. One of the best overtakes of the season, at the expense of one of the most talented (if not the most) drivers on the grid, on a track that is not so friendly to overtakes.

## The second stops

After every race contender had pitted (except Antonelli), the McLarens, the Ferraris and Max were stuck behind Hadjar, who had not pitted yet. With the first pit window over, the lap 1 positions had been restored. On lap 30 Ferrari decided to pit Hamilton early as an opportunity to hunt even the first place, to which McLaren responded by pitting the leader of the race and freeing space for Norris to run outside his teammate's dirty air, which he was complaining about in the team radios.

## The contact that cost Piastri the lead

On lap 38 Oscar was starting to find pace with his fresh tyres and clearing some backmarkers, when [Carlos Sainz](/drivers/carlos-sainz) didn't let Piastri through, which resulted in contact between the cars and the Spaniard getting a 5-second penalty. By staying out while Piastri stopped, Norris came out in front of his teammate and took the title of the favourite to win the race.

## Piastri's race unravels

The other [Cadillac](/teams/cadillac) was forced to be the second retirement of the race after [Perez](/drivers/sergio-perez) broke the front left suspension, but the Mexican was able to return it to the garage without the need for a VSC. After the incident with Sainz, harmful for his possibilities for the race win, disaster struck again for the young Australian driver as his gearbox betrayed him and forced him to retire from what was about to be his best race this season.

## The VSC, and a penalty for Hamilton

This incident resulted in a VSC where the leader, Norris, pitted, but he had created a gap big enough to stay in the lead. Hamilton pitted too and rejoined in front of the championship leader, but the Ferrari pit wall saw that at the safety car line the Italian was in front and informed Lewis to hand the position back, instructions that he followed. But this was not the only time rules were used against the 7-time world champion, as he was penalized with a 5-second penalty for speeding in the pit lane; this is the third consecutive race that Lewis gets a penalty.

## The final order

At the end Lando Norris got his first win as a world champion, and deservingly so. Max Verstappen got his second consecutive podium as Kimi Antonelli completed the podium in a race where he extended his [championship lead](/series/f1/standings). Ferrari 4-5 with Leclerc at P4 because of Hamilton's penalty, the other [Red Bull](/teams/red-bull-racing) finished in 6th place as Russell recovered from his bad start to P7. Double points for [Racing Bulls](/teams/racing-bulls) as their drivers finished in P8 and P10, and [Nico Hulkenberg](/drivers/nico-hulkenberg) got his first points in an [Audi](/teams/audi) uniform after he finished in 9th place. For the out-of-the-points notable stuff, [Aston Martin](/teams/aston-martin) with their 16 upgrades finished 13-14, very promising for the remainder of the season. And the race for the 5th place in the constructors seems to slip away from [Alpine](/teams/alpine), which ended the race pointless. ([Full classification](/series/f1/results).)
