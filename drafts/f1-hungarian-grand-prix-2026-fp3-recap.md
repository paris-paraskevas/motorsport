# The hiding is over: Norris tops final practice as McLaren finally show their hand

We flagged it on Friday: eleventh in first practice for the reigning champion, a raised eyebrow rather than a red pen, because [McLaren](/teams/mclaren) hiding on a Friday and then finding half a second on Saturday is the sport's oldest routine. Right on schedule, [Lando Norris](/drivers/lando-norris) put the [McLaren](/teams/mclaren) on top of final practice, a 1:17.939 that left [Lewis Hamilton](/drivers/lewis-hamilton)'s [Ferrari](/teams/ferrari) 0.117 behind and the rest of the front-runners bunched up in his wake.

## The order arrives

[Kimi Antonelli](/drivers/kimi-antonelli) slotted third for [Mercedes](/teams/mercedes), a hundredth off Hamilton, with [Charles Leclerc](/drivers/charles-leclerc) fourth and [Oscar Piastri](/drivers/oscar-piastri) fifth to keep both Ferraris and both McLarens inside the top five. [George Russell](/drivers/george-russell) was sixth. The notable name lower down was [Max Verstappen](/drivers/max-verstappen), seventh and seven tenths adrift, the [Red Bull](/teams/red-bull-racing) looking like exactly the handful its driver keeps describing at a track that punishes a car for being anything less than planted.

## What it told us

Not much that Friday had not already hinted at, and everything the paddock suspected. The new floor McLaren brought to fix their low-speed-corner problem looked like it had done its job; the car that could barely see the road in the morning session was suddenly the one to beat once the track came to it. Ferrari, quick all weekend through the corners and short on the straights, stayed in the fight. The final hour before qualifying rarely lies, and this one pointed squarely at a McLaren-Ferrari front row. Whether the low-fuel headline pace survived into the session that counts was, as ever at the Hungaroring, the only question that mattered.
