# Belgian Grand Prix 2026, lap by lap: the Spa race in order

The result was [Kimi Antonelli](/drivers/kimi-antonelli) from [Charles Leclerc](/drivers/charles-leclerc) and [Max Verstappen](/drivers/max-verstappen), under two seconds covering the lead pair. The route there ran through two safety-car phases, two virtual ones, three retirements, four lead changes and a recovery drive from the back to sixth. In order:

## Lap 1
[Verstappen](/drivers/max-verstappen) tows past Antonelli into Raidillon; Antonelli repasses up the Kemmel Straight. Behind, the midfield is already scrapping: [Franco Colapinto](/drivers/franco-colapinto) picks off [Pierre Gasly](/drivers/pierre-gasly) and [Gabriel Bortoleto](/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto), [Liam Lawson](/drivers/liam-lawson) takes Bortoleto too, and [Lando Norris](/drivers/lando-norris), starting 13th on a grid penalty, is already moving. [George Russell](/drivers/george-russell) loses energy out of La Source, tumbles through the order, and is collected by [Lewis Hamilton](/drivers/lewis-hamilton) at Turn 6. Out. Safety car.

## Laps 1-4: safety car
Field neutralised while Russell's [Mercedes](/teams/mercedes) is recovered. Hamilton takes a five-second penalty for the contact.

## Laps 5-17: green, and a charge through the field
Racing resumes and [Isack Hadjar](/drivers/isack-hadjar), starting from the back, begins the drive of the day: Esteban Ocon, [Valtteri Bottas](/drivers/valtteri-bottas) and [Sergio Pérez](/drivers/sergio-perez) on lap 5, [Lance Stroll](/drivers/lance-stroll) on lap 6, [Oliver Bearman](/drivers/oliver-bearman) on lap 10, [Carlos Sainz](/drivers/carlos-sainz) on lap 11, [Alexander Albon](/drivers/alexander-albon) on lap 12, [Nico Hülkenberg](/drivers/nico-hulkenberg) on lap 13, and Bortoleto for the top ten on lap 15. Colapinto and Norris trade eighth on lap 8. Pérez retires on lap 13 with suspension failure. Up front, Antonelli still leads Leclerc.

## Laps 18-23: the virtual safety car swing
- **L18-21:** two VSC periods. [Ferrari](/teams/ferrari) times its stop to perfection.
- **L19:** Leclerc emerges ahead of Antonelli. New leader. Behind, Lawson and Gasly start a fight that runs for a dozen laps, swapping places on laps 19 and 21.
- **L21:** Norris, offset on strategy, leads.
- **L23:** Leclerc repasses Norris and pulls clear, three seconds in hand.

## Laps 24-33: Leclerc controls
Leclerc leads in clean air; Antonelli, on the harder tyre, reels him in. Stroll retires on lap 25, gearbox. Sainz clears Ocon for 16th on lap 24. Colapinto lights up the midfield, taking Hülkenberg on lap 28 and a Häkkinen-style double into Les Combes on lap 29, then Lawson on lap 30. Lawson and Gasly are still at it, swapping again on laps 30 and 33.

## Lap 34: the winning move
Antonelli takes the lead back from Leclerc at Les Combes. Clean, no contact. It is the last lead change of the race.

## Laps 35-44: managed home
Antonelli controls the gap to the flag, under two seconds. Hamilton passes [Oscar Piastri](/drivers/oscar-piastri) late for fourth, despite his penalty and an unsafe-release investigation.

## Pit log (28 stops)
A one-stop race for most. Antonelli: medium to lap 18, hard to the end. Norris: 13th on the grid from a power-unit penalty, then a 7.8-second stop, recovered to seventh. Hadjar: from the back of the grid to sixth.

## Retirements
- **L1** Russell (Mercedes), lap-one contact after an energy failure.
- **L13** Pérez ([Cadillac](/teams/cadillac)), suspension.
- **L25** Stroll ([Aston Martin](/teams/aston-martin)), gearbox.
