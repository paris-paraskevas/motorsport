# One hour to learn a goodbye: Antonelli leads the only practice, Russell steals sprint pole

Formula 1 came back from four weeks off and gave itself sixty minutes to work out a circuit it will never come back to. That is the arithmetic of a sprint weekend, and [Zandvoort](/series/f1/weekend/12) got the fifth of this season's six for the last event it will ever hold. One hour of practice, then parc fermé shuts, then everything counts. Whatever the setup sheets said at the end of that hour is roughly what twenty-two cars raced all weekend.

[Kimi Antonelli](/drivers/kimi-antonelli) read it quickest. A 1:12.949 was the only lap all session under 1m13s, and it landed in the closing minutes on his second flying run on softs after traffic ruined the first. He had already spun at the exit of Turn 10 by then, which he explained over the radio with admirable economy: "I don't know what happened there." The championship leader, half a second of drama and then the fastest lap of the day. It is becoming a habit.

## The order nobody had time to argue with

[Lando Norris](/drivers/lando-norris) ended up second at +0.121, [George Russell](/drivers/george-russell) third at +0.125. Four thousandths of a second between a McLaren and the second Mercedes, which on a Friday means nothing and by Saturday evening meant everything. [Lewis Hamilton](/drivers/lewis-hamilton) was fourth and [Charles Leclerc](/drivers/charles-leclerc) fifth, so [Ferrari](/teams/ferrari) had both cars in the top five before the weekend had properly started, and [Oscar Piastri](/drivers/oscar-piastri) was sixth at +0.659, the first driver more than half a second adrift.

The session had opened wet. The track was declared as such, damp patches were scattered around the lap, and the early runners went out on intermediates before it dried underneath them fast enough that Piastri was on mediums five minutes in. Rain was threatening again by the end. [The Race called it](https://www.the-race.com/formula-1/what-happened-in-only-dutch-gp-f1-2026-practice-session/) exactly what it was: not enough running, in the wrong conditions, before the part that mattered.

Nobody had a worse hour than [Carlos Sainz](/drivers/carlos-sainz). Into the gravel at Turn 13, then wide again at Turn 11, floor damage on the [Williams](/teams/williams), and last of everybody at +3.396. It cost him more than a Friday: the repairs pushed Williams into overnight suspension changes, the changes broke parc fermé, and [Sainz started the sprint from the pit lane](https://www.gpblog.com/en/breaking-news/pit-lane-start-for-sainz-as-williams-breaks-parc-ferme-to-reset-difficult-dutch-gp-weekend) as a result. One excursion, one weekend gone.

## Audi did the clever thing

Look at seventh and ninth on that sheet. [Nico Hulkenberg](/drivers/nico-hulkenberg) at +0.835 and [Gabriel Bortoleto](/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto) at +1.043, and both of them set those times on the **medium** compound while the cars around them were on softs. On a weekend with one practice session, that is a team that arrived knowing what it wanted and spent the hour confirming it rather than searching. [Audi](/teams/audi) have not had many afternoons like that this year.

It did not last, which is the cruel part. Hulkenberg's sprint ended after seven laps with a power unit failure, and the car that had looked the most composed thing on the circuit on Friday lunchtime never got to show it.

## Eleventh, at his own farewell

[Max Verstappen](/drivers/max-verstappen) has won this race three times since it returned in 2021 and finished second in the other two. It is the best record anyone has at the modern Zandvoort by a distance, this is the last Dutch Grand Prix the circuit will host, and he ended the only practice session of the weekend eleventh, 1.376s off the pace, after running wide at Turn 13.

Red Bull arrived rearranged too. [Liam Lawson](/drivers/liam-lawson) was in the second car because [Isack Hadjar](/drivers/isack-hadjar) is out, the Frenchman having [reportedly fractured his wrist](https://www.planetf1.com/news/isack-hadjar-wrist-injury-fresh-details-dutch-gp) on a heavy bag in the gym over the break, which is a sentence that should not appear in a Formula 1 season report and yet here we are. It costs [Red Bull](/teams/red-bull-racing) a driver sitting eighth in the [championship](/series/f1/standings) on 68 points. Lawson had never driven the 2026 car and was handed it on a weekend containing one hour of practice; he was fourteenth. Yuki Tsunoda, who started the year without a seat anywhere, came back at [Racing Bulls](/teams/racing-bulls) and was seventeenth.

## Then Russell produced a lap out of nowhere

Sprint qualifying is where Friday stopped being preparation. Russell had topped neither segment: Leclerc headed the first, Piastri the second, and Russell was somewhere behind both. With four minutes left Norris went out into clean air and took provisional pole, and then Russell answered with a **1:11.567** and beat it by **0.041s**.

Behind those two, Leclerc was third at +0.055 and Piastri fourth at +0.099. The whole front two rows of the sprint grid inside a tenth of a second. [Motorsport.com described it](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/f1-dutch-gp-sprint-qualifying-report/10847946/) as a tight sprint pole and that undersells it: at a circuit with this reputation for processional racing, Zandvoort's only sprint grid was set by four hundredths.

It was Russell's third sprint pole of the season from five sprint weekends, after China and Canada, and he would go on to convert this one the way he converted China. [Sky's framing](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/12433/13575268/dutch-gp-sprint-qualifying-george-russell-claims-pole-position-with-title-rivals-kimi-antonelli-lewis-hamilton-on-back-foot) was the useful one: his title rivals were on the back foot, and one of them was his team-mate.

## Antonelli broke his own Friday

The championship leader's afternoon fell apart before most people noticed. He slid off into the gravel at the penultimate corner during the first phase of sprint qualifying, carried floor damage out of it, and could only manage fifth on his final run at +0.227. Fifth is not a disaster. Fifth with a damaged floor, at the circuit where he collected two penalty points last year for putting Leclerc into a barrier, on a weekend where second place in the championship is genuinely in play behind him, is a Friday he will want back.

Verstappen was sixth, more than half a second off Russell. [Aston Martin](/teams/aston-martin) had the worst of it: new Honda hardware, a scrappy hour, [Fernando Alonso](/drivers/fernando-alonso) locking up at Turn 1 and running wide, and twenty-second on the sheet. He too would start the sprint from the pit lane, for [the same parc fermé breach as Williams](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/sainz-alonso-start-pit-lane-094020064.html).

## What Friday actually told us

Two things, and they pointed in different directions. [Mercedes](/teams/mercedes) had the fastest car and the fastest single lap, and they belonged to different drivers, which is a good problem until it is a points problem. And the field was so tightly packed after one hour of running that the sprint grid came down to hundredths, which is not what anyone expects from a circuit whose great criticism is that nothing happens on it.

**For the books:** Zandvoort's first and only sprint qualifying session, the last Friday of Formula 1 the circuit will ever stage, Lawson's first Friday in a 2026 Red Bull, and a 1:12.949 that stood as the only sub-1m13s lap of the day.

One hour to learn a circuit, and the two men who used it best did not end up on the same row. Zandvoort was never going to go quietly.
