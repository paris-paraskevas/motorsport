# Ferrari giving fans their dose of hopium, Antonelli's return to dominance: Belgium's Spa never disappoints

Surviving [the Belgian Grand Prix](/series/f1/weekend/10): not what [Lewis Hamilton](/drivers/lewis-hamilton) aimed for on Sunday morning. Somehow, as [Ferrari](/teams/ferrari) always do, Hamilton managed to: crash into ex-teammate [George Russell](/drivers/george-russell) on the opening lap, serve a 5 second penalty in a 10.6 second pit stop, run over a mechanic, and finish fourth. The mechanic was unhurt, Ferrari swallowed a fine for the unsafe release, and the whole thing still ended in points. A usual Ferrari disaster-class.

## The rocketship and the knife

It would be a shame not to talk about [Kimi Antonelli](/drivers/kimi-antonelli)'s ruthless competitiveness, paired with yet another of Toto's rocketships, which run circles around the entire grid when they don't decide to break down or have reliability issues. Pole on Saturday, his sixth of the year. Win on Sunday, also his sixth of the year. That is not a hot streak. That is a habit.

## The rich stay rich

The new regulations punish some, award others. [Cadillac](/teams/cadillac) and [Aston Martin](/teams/aston-martin) suffered a mechanical DNF apiece, as they have become accustomed to doing: suspension for [Sergio Pérez](/drivers/sergio-perez) on lap 13, gearbox for [Lance Stroll](/drivers/lance-stroll) on lap 25. [Mercedes](/teams/mercedes), Ferrari, [McLaren](/teams/mclaren) and [Red Bull](/teams/red-bull-racing) spent yet another race well ahead of those behind.

## Kemmel theatre

For us the viewers, some outstanding overtakes and defences on the Kemmel Straight; most notable of the former being [Franco Colapinto](/drivers/franco-colapinto)'s Häkkinen-esque double overtake over his own teammate [Pierre Gasly](/drivers/pierre-gasly) and rival [Liam Lawson](/drivers/liam-lawson). [Charles Leclerc](/drivers/charles-leclerc) held the "too close for comfort" defence over [Oscar Piastri](/drivers/oscar-piastri), sharing a little contact going into Les Combes. Piastri's floor paid the bill; the stewards shrugged. Colapinto's double, for the record, was the best thing an Argentine did all Sunday.

## Russell breaks, Verstappen returns

Frankly, an entertaining race, and no complaints to be had for anyone other than George Russell, who faced yet another reliability issue: no power and no battery charge leaving La Source on lap 1. He called it dangerous; Toto Wolff called it "100 per cent on us". Neither was wrong. In contrast to the Brit's devastating result, the Dutchman [Max Verstappen](/drivers/max-verstappen) returned to the podium's third step now that his "Macarena" wing is gone and his beloved high-downforce-on-a-high-speed-track setup is back.

## He had to win it twice

Leclerc and Antonelli proved they were in their own stratosphere, though we must note that Leclerc did win out with a well timed pit stop under the virtual safety car on lap 21, and for a dozen laps Spa dared to pretend it had a title fight. Then Antonelli hunted the Ferrari down, re-passed it into Les Combes on lap 34, and cleared off: 1.952 seconds at the flag, six wins from ten, and a [45 point lead](/series/f1/standings) that is starting to read like a verdict.

## Recovery school

As far as recovery drives go, [Isack Hadjar](/drivers/isack-hadjar) and [Lando Norris](/drivers/lando-norris) executed them beautifully. The French prodigy punished all the underdeveloped midfield and tractor teams, coming from the back of the grid to land himself a respectable points haul in sixth. And McLaren, fortunately, failed at sabotaging the reigning world champion with a 7.8 second pit stop (a left-rear that refused to leave), as Norris's consistent overdrive placed him seventh at the chequered flag. He reckons the stop and the safety-car timing cost him 16 seconds and a podium. McLaren, painfully, agree.

Two-time champion rookie [Fernando Alonso](/drivers/fernando-alonso) spent yet another race watching the fast boys fly by, blue flags dominating his afternoon on the way to 19th, last of the classified runners and two laps down. (At least Spain's national football team brought him some much needed happiness later that night.)

## Where it leaves us

**Top ten:** 1. Antonelli (Mercedes), 2. Leclerc (Ferrari), 3. Verstappen (Red Bull), 4. Hamilton (Ferrari), 5. Piastri (McLaren), 6. Hadjar (Red Bull), 7. Norris (McLaren), 8. Bortoleto (Audi), 9. Lindblad (Racing Bulls), 10. Colapinto (Alpine). ([Full classification](/series/f1/results).)

Antonelli leaves Belgium on 204 points. Hamilton's recovery to fourth makes him the closest challenger on 159, 45 behind, with Russell third on 154 and now 50 adrift of the teammate he is supposed to be racing. Leclerc (126) and Norris (103) complete the top five. Formula 1 goes again next weekend at the [Hungarian Grand Prix](/series/f1/weekend/11) in Budapest, and after that the grid gets a month to lick its wounds.
