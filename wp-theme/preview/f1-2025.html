Six turning points

Numbered as on the chart

  1. 1
    Saudi Arabian Grand PrixRound 5 · 20 Apr

    Piastri wins and takes the lead from Norris by 10 points. Norris crashed in qualifying, started tenth and finished fourth.

    After itPiastri 99Norris 89Verstappen 87

  2. 2
    Dutch Grand PrixRound 15 · 31 Aug

    Norris's car fails a few laps from the end and Piastri wins to lead by 34. Verstappen, second in the race, is 104 points behind, his furthest of the year.

    After itPiastri 309Norris 275Verstappen 205

  3. 3
    Azerbaijan Grand PrixRound 17 · 21 Sep

    Piastri jumps the start and crashes out on the opening lap, his first retirement of the year. Verstappen wins again and Norris cuts the lead to 25.

    After itPiastri 324Norris 299Verstappen 255

  4. 4
    Mexico City Grand PrixRound 20 · 26 Oct

    Norris wins from pole while Piastri finishes fifth, and the lead changes hands for the second time, by one point.

    After itNorris 357Piastri 356Verstappen 321

  5. 5
    Las Vegas Grand PrixRound 22 · 22 Nov

    Both McLarens are disqualified, their skid blocks worn below the 9 mm minimum. Verstappen wins and draws level with Piastri, 24 behind Norris.

    After itNorris 390Piastri 366Verstappen 366

    Abu Dhabi Grand PrixRound 24 · 7 Dec

    Verstappen wins and Piastri is second, but third place is enough for Norris: champion by two points.

The decider

Abu Dhabi Grand Prix · 7 December

Going in

  1. 1Norris408
  2. 2Verstappen396 12 behind
  3. 3Piastri392 16 behind

In Qatar a week before, McLaren left both cars out under an early safety car; Verstappen pitted and won. One Grand Prix and 25 points were left.

What each needed

Norris
A podium, whatever the others did.
Verstappen
To win, with Norris fourth or lower.
Piastri
To win with Norris sixth or lower, or to be second with Norris tenth or lower and Verstappen off the podium.

As formula1.com set out the permutations before the race.

What happened

  1. 1Verstappen421 in all
  2. 2Piastri410 in all
  3. 3Norris423 champion

The three in the race's order, with each one's points for the season. Norris won the title by two points, Verstappen's run of four titles in a row ended, and McLaren had already taken the teams' title in Singapore.
