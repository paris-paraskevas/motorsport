The **2026 IndyCar season** runs **18 races** from **March to September**, with an even split of **six ovals, six street circuits and six road courses** — a calendar that shows off the series' all-rounder character.

Everything is built around the **110th Running of the Indianapolis 500**, held in **May** at the Indianapolis Motor Speedway — the milestone edition of the sport's biggest race and, as ever, the centrepiece of the year.

There is fresh ground on the schedule. Two brand-new **street races** join the calendar — one at **Arlington, Texas**, the other in **Washington, D.C.** The Canadian round also changes: rather than returning to Toronto, it moves to a **new street circuit at Markham**, Ontario. And **Milwaukee** steps up to an **oval double-header** — two points-paying races across one weekend.
