The **2025–26** campaign is **Season 12** of Formula E — and the longest in its history, with a record **seventeen races**. The calendar opens in **São Paulo** and closes with a **London double-header** finale.

It is also the **final season of the Gen3 Evo car** before the new **Gen4** regulations arrive for 2026–27, making this the last year of the current all-electric machinery.

The grid gains a new manufacturer name: **Citroën** joins Formula E for the first time, taking over the Stellantis-owned entry that previously raced as Maserati.

There are changes at both ends of the map, too:

- **Madrid** is a new addition, appearing on the Formula E calendar for the first time.
- **Miami** moves to a new venue — the Miami International Autodrome, home of the Formula 1 Miami Grand Prix.

With a record-length season, a manufacturer debut and the Gen3 Evo era drawing to a close before Gen4, Season 12 shapes up as a milestone year — the last chapter of one car generation before the next begins.
