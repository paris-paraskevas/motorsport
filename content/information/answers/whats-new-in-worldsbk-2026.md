The **2026** WorldSBK season spans **12 rounds**, opening at **Phillip Island** in Australia in **February** and closing at **Jerez** in Spain in **October**.

The headline storyline is a wide-open championship. For the **first time in 13 years**, the grid features **no reigning champion**: **Toprak Razgatlıoğlu**, who won the 2025 title, has moved up to **MotoGP** for 2026. With the most recent champion gone, the crown is there for the taking, and the title fight is expected to be one of the most open in years.

There is also a change lower down the ladder. Alongside the established **WorldSSP** middleweight class, **2026** introduces a new entry-level **Sportbike** World Championship, broadening the racing on a typical WorldSBK weekend and adding another rung to the junior ladder.
