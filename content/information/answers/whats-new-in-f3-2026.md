The **2026 FIA Formula 3 Championship** runs **nine rounds** from **March to September**, most of them held on **Formula 1 race weekends** as a support category.

The headline calendar change is in Spain: Formula 3 follows Formula 1 in **dropping the Imola round** and adding Madrid's **newly built "Madring"** circuit, which also stages the **season finale** in September. The campaign opens in March at **Albert Park in Melbourne**, alongside the Australian Grand Prix.

As ever, **teams field three cars each**, so the grids are large and the racing is close. With so many talented drivers on a fixed, spec car, standing out is hard — and the reward for doing so is the prize every F3 driver is chasing: promotion to **Formula 2** and a step closer to Formula 1.

For how F3 fits into the wider junior ladder, see [F1 vs F2 vs F3](/information/feeder-series/difference-between-f1-f2-f3).
