The **2026 GT World Challenge Europe** season runs **ten rounds from April to October**, split evenly into **five Sprint Cup and five Endurance Cup** weekends, with combined results deciding the overall title.

As ever, the year is built around the **24 Hours of Spa** in late June — the sport's greatest GT3 race and the single most valuable round on the calendar.

The headline change is the return of **Portimão**. The Algarve International Circuit closes the season as its **endurance finale**, its first appearance on the calendar in **over a decade** — and its first ever as an **Endurance Cup** round, having previously featured only in the Sprint Cup.

Otherwise the calendar stays largely stable, taking in established European venues, while the global **GT World Challenge** title continues to be fought across the **European, American and Asian** arms of the series.
