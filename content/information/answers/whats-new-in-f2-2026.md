The **2026 season** brings Formula 2 a notably new-world flavour. It runs **14 rounds** alongside Formula 1, opening in **Melbourne in March** and closing in **Abu Dhabi in December**.

The headline is F2's first trip to **North America**: **Miami** and **Montreal** join as the championship's first North American rounds. Madrid's brand-new **"Madring"** circuit also enters the calendar for the first time, giving Spain a second date after Barcelona.

The season is a milestone in its own right — the **tenth** run under the FIA Formula 2 name since the series launched in 2017. As ever, the grid is a spec field of identical cars, so the results are all about the drivers.

That matters because F2 is the championship that graduates its stars **straight to Formula 1** — the order here is a preview of who is next in line for a Grand Prix seat.
