---
title: GT World Challenge — overview
---

GT World Challenge is SRO's global **GT3** competition — one class of production-based supercars, split by driver rating into Pro, Gold, Silver and Bronze so amateurs and professionals share the same grid. Its European series is the strongest GT3 field in the world; sister championships run in America and Asia.

## Race weekend shape

The European calendar splits into two cups: a **Sprint Cup** of one-hour races in pairs, and an **Endurance Cup** of longer races — 3, 6 and 24 hours — shared by three or four drivers. Combined results across both decide the overall title, and the **24 Hours of Spa** is the centrepiece of the whole season.

## Points

A race win pays **25 points**, sliding to 1 for tenth, scored within each class; sprint pole adds a bonus. The flagship enduros carry more weight — Spa awards points at the 6-, 12- and 24-hour marks.

## What to watch for in 2026

Ten European rounds run from April to October, built around the Spa 24 Hours in June. **Portimão returns** as the season's endurance finale for the first time in over a decade, and the global GT World Challenge title is still fought across the European, American and Asian arms.
