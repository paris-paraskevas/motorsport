---
title: IndyCar — overview
---

IndyCar is the premier US open-wheel championship — a genuine all-rounder that races **ovals, street circuits and road courses** in one season. Every team runs the spec Dallara IR-18 with a hybrid-assisted 2.2-litre twin-turbo V6 from **Honda or Chevrolet**, so it comes down to driver and team execution.

## Race weekend shape

A weekend is practice, qualifying and race. Road and street courses use a multi-round knockout ending in the **Firestone Fast Six** shootout for pole; ovals qualify on raw speed. The **Indianapolis 500** — 200 laps, 500 miles — has its own multi-day qualifying and stands apart as the series' crown jewel.

## Points

A win pays **50 points**, then 40 for second, 35 for third and a sliding scale down the field. Pole is worth **1 point** (except the Indy 500, where the Fast 12 score 12 down to 1); leading a lap earns a point, and leading the most earns two.

## What to watch for in 2026

Eighteen races run from March to September — an even split of six ovals, six streets and six road courses — built around the **110th Indianapolis 500** in May. New street races at **Arlington and Washington, D.C.** join the calendar, the Canadian round shifts from Toronto to a new street circuit at Markham, and Milwaukee runs an oval double-header.
