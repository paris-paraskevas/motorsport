---
title: Formula 2 — overview
---

Formula 2 is the FIA's feeder championship one rung below Formula 1 — a **spec** series where every team runs the same Dallara chassis, Mecachrome V6 turbo and Pirelli tyres, so results come down to the driver rather than the budget. It supports the F1 calendar, racing on Grand Prix weekends.

## Race weekend shape

One practice and one qualifying set up **two races**: a **Sprint** on Saturday, its grid formed by reversing the top ten of qualifying, and a longer **Feature** on Sunday from the full qualifying order. The Feature carries a mandatory pit stop; the Sprint doesn't.

## Points

The Feature pays the top ten **25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1**; the Sprint pays the top eight **10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1**. Qualifying earns the Feature polesitter **2 points**, and there's **1 point** for fastest lap in each race (top-ten finish required) — a 39-point maximum weekend.

## What to watch for in 2026

Fourteen rounds run from March to December alongside F1. The calendar tilts new-world for the first time: **Miami and Montreal** come in as F2's first North American rounds, and Madrid's new "Madring" joins too. This is the category that graduates drivers straight to Formula 1, so the order here is a preview of who's next.
