---
title: FIA WEC — overview
---

The FIA World Endurance Championship, run jointly with the Automobile Club de l'Ouest, is top-flight sportscar racing — multi-driver crews in timed enduros across two classes: **Hypercar** at the front and **LMGT3** for production-based GT cars. Its flagship is the **24 Hours of Le Mans**.

## Race weekend shape

Eight rounds, each a timed endurance race — five six-hour events plus Le Mans (24 hours), Qatar (1812km) and Bahrain (8 hours). Hypercar and LMGT3 run at the same time but score their own classifications, and each car is shared by two or three drivers.

## Points

Points go to the top ten in each class — **25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1** at a six-hour race, with the longer rounds weighted heavier and **Le Mans paying double**. There's a bonus point for pole, and a manufacturer has to contest every round to be eligible for the title.

## What to watch for in 2026

Eight rounds run from Imola in April to Bahrain in November, with Le Mans in June. The Hypercar grid is unusually deep — **eight manufacturers**, including new entrant **Genesis**, the first South Korean marque in the top class — though Porsche's factory programme has stepped away.
