---
title: Formula E — overview
---

Formula E is the FIA's all-electric World Championship — the only one of its kind — racing spec **Gen3 Evo** cars almost entirely on temporary **street circuits** in city centres. All-wheel drive and up to 350kW make it the fastest-accelerating single-seater the FIA has homologated.

## Race weekend shape

Mostly single-day events: practice, then a **knockout qualifying** where the field splits into two groups before the top four from each fight one-on-one **Duels** for pole. The race runs to a fixed duration rather than a set number of laps. **Attack Mode** — an extra burst of power armed by driving through an off-line activation zone — is mandatory, and at "Pit Boost" rounds drivers also take a mandatory in-race fast-charge stop.

## Points

The race pays the top ten **25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1**, with **3 points** for pole and **1 point** for fastest lap (top-ten finish required).

## What to watch for in 2026

This is Season 12 and the longest calendar yet — **seventeen races**, opening in São Paulo and closing with a London double-header. It's the **final season of the Gen3 Evo car** before Gen4 arrives, with Citroën joining the grid and Madrid new on the calendar.
