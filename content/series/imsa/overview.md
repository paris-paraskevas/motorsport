---
title: IMSA — overview
---

The IMSA SportsCar Championship is North America's top sportscar series, racing four classes together: the **GTP** prototypes at the front, **LMP2**, and the production-based **GTD Pro** and **GTD** GT3 cars. Multi-driver crews contest a mix of sprints and endurance classics.

## Race weekend shape

Eleven rounds blend short races with big enduros — headlined by the **Rolex 24 At Daytona** and the **12 Hours of Sebring**. Every class races for its own title, and the five longest races also count toward a separate **Michelin Endurance Cup**.

## Points

Each round awards championship points by class for the season-long titles. The Michelin Endurance Cup is scored separately within the long races — points banked at intervals through the race, not just at the flag — so pace and reliability across the enduros are rewarded in their own right.

## What to watch for in 2026

The season opens with its signature back-to-back classics — Daytona in January, Sebring in March — across eleven rounds to October. The GTP class fields **five manufacturers** (Porsche, Cadillac, BMW, Acura and Aston Martin), with Aston's Valkyrie the outlier on non-hybrid Le Mans rules — though Acura has announced it will leave GTP at the end of the year.
