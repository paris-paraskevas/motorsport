---
title: NLS Nürburgring — overview
---

The Nürburgring Langstrecken-Serie (NLS) — the long-running series formerly known as the VLN — is endurance racing run at the **Nürburgring**, on the combined Grand Prix circuit and the legendary Nordschleife — more than 24 kilometres a lap. A single enormous grid mixes professionals and amateurs across many classes, from **GT3** at the front through GT4, prototypes and production cars to touring machinery. It is not the same event as the standalone ADAC Nürburgring 24 Hours.

## Race weekend shape

Ten races a season, most of them **four hours** long (one runs to six), run by one to three drivers — a single driver may take the four-hour races solo. The series doubles as the traditional proving ground for the Nürburgring 24 Hours — two of its rounds serve as the official 24-hour qualifiers.

## Points

Championships are decided **by class**, not outright: points come from finishing position within your class, the length of the race and the number of starters, with each entrant's best eight of ten results counting. A separate overall trophy rewards the fastest cars on the day.

## What to watch for in 2026

Ten rounds run from March to October at the Nürburgring, including the 24-hour qualifier weekend in April. As ever, the appeal is the sheer variety — a hundred-plus cars of wildly different speeds sharing one of the longest, most demanding circuits in the world.
